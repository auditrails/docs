# Compliance Mapping

AuditRails is purpose-built for compliance. All 18 supported frameworks are active today, bundled free on Compliance Trails. This guide maps our features to specific requirements across the 10 broadest frameworks in depth; EU AI Act (our deepest integration, covering Articles 12/14/50/72/73) and the 7 country-specific frameworks are summarized at the end.

## SOC 2 Type II

SOC 2 requires demonstrating controls over security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

| SOC 2 Criteria | AuditRails Feature |
|----------------|-------------------|
| CC6.1 — Logical access controls | Log `access.granted`, `access.revoked`, `auth.login`, `auth.failed` |
| CC6.2 — Access authentication | Log `auth.login`, `auth.logout` with MFA metadata |
| CC6.3 — Access authorization | Log `permission.granted`, `permission.revoked` with role details |
| CC7.2 — Monitor for anomalies | Dashboard search + export for audit review |
| CC7.3 — Evaluate security events | Real-time log search, action filtering |
| CC8.1 — Change management | Log `config.changed`, `deploy.completed` |
| A1.2 — Recovery mechanisms | WORM storage ensures log availability even after incidents |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for SOC 2:**
- **Immutable audit trail** — hash-chained logs satisfy evidence retention requirements
- **WORM storage** — S3 Object Lock meets SEC 17a-4 style requirements
- **RBAC** — Auditor role provides read-only access for external auditors
- **CSV export** — Generate evidence packages for audit periods

## HIPAA

HIPAA requires logging access to Protected Health Information (PHI) and maintaining audit controls.

| HIPAA Requirement | AuditRails Feature |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| §164.312(b) — Audit controls | Log all PHI access: `phi.accessed`, `phi.disclosed`, `phi.amended` |
| §164.312(c) — Integrity controls | Hash chaining detects any log tampering |
| §164.312(d) — Person authentication | Log `auth.login`, `auth.failed` with actor identification |
| §164.308(a)(1) — Risk analysis | Compliance dashboard tracks security control completion |
| §164.308(a)(5) — Security awareness | Track `training.completed` events |
| §164.408 — Breach notification | Log `breach.detected`, `breach.notified` with timelines |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for HIPAA:**
- **Tamper-proof logs** — hash chains prove logs haven't been altered
- **Access logging** — track every PHI access with actor, resource, and timestamp
- **Retention** — subscribing to HIPAA (on Framework Trails or Compliance Trails) sets your retention to its required 6 years
- **Encryption** — data encrypted at rest (S3 SSE) and in transit (TLS 1.3)

## NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555)

The NIS2 Directive requires essential and important entities across the EU to implement cybersecurity risk management and incident reporting.

| NIS2 Requirement | AuditRails Feature |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Art. 21 — Risk-management measures | Immutable audit trail of security events, access controls, and configuration changes |
| Art. 21(2)(d) — Supply chain security | Log `access.granted` with third-party identification for supplier access auditing |
| Art. 21(2)(h) — Cryptography & encryption | SHA-256 hash chaining, WORM storage, TLS 1.3 in transit, SSE at rest |
| Art. 21(2)(i) — Access control & authentication | Log `auth.login`, `auth.failed`, `auth.mfa`, `access.granted`, `access.revoked` |
| Art. 23 — Incident reporting (24h/72h/1mo) | Log `incident.detected`, `incident.notified`, `incident.resolved` with timestamps |
| Art. 20 — Governance & management oversight | Compliance dashboard tracks control completion, `training.completed` events |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for NIS2:**
- **Incident timeline** — timestamped logs prove compliance with 24-hour early warning and 72-hour notification deadlines
- **Supply chain auditing** — track third-party access with actor identification and scope metadata
- **Hash-chained integrity** — cryptographic proof that audit logs have not been tampered with (Art. 21(2)(h))
- **5-year retention** — meets NIS2 record-keeping expectations with WORM storage

## DORA — Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU 2022/2554)

DORA requires financial entities to implement comprehensive ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, and third-party risk management.

| DORA Requirement | AuditRails Feature |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Art. 5-9 — ICT risk management framework | Immutable audit trail of ICT operations, access controls, and system changes |
| Art. 6 — Logging of ICT operations | Hash-chained logging of all activities with actor, action, resource, timestamp |
| Art. 17-19 — Incident management & reporting | Log `incident.detected`, `incident.notified`, `incident.resolved` with timelines |
| Art. 24-27 — Resilience testing | Log `vulnerability.scan` events with findings and severity tracking |
| Art. 28-30 — Third-party ICT risk | Log `access.granted` with third-party actor identification and scope |
| Art. 5 — Management body oversight | Compliance dashboard tracks control completion, `training.completed` events |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for DORA:**
- **ICT operations logging** — comprehensive, hash-chained audit trail of all system activities (Art. 6)
- **Incident timelines** — timestamped logs prove compliance with 4-hour initial, 72-hour intermediate, and 1-month final reporting deadlines
- **Third-party monitoring** — track ICT provider access with actor identification and audit rights
- **5-year retention** — meets DORA record-keeping requirements with WORM storage

## CCPA/CPRA — California Consumer Privacy Act

The CCPA/CPRA grants California consumers rights over their personal information and requires businesses to maintain audit trails of data processing.

| CCPA/CPRA Requirement | AuditRails Feature |
|-----------------------|-------------------|
| §1798.100 — Right to Know | Log `data.accessed` with actor, resource, purpose, and data categories |
| §1798.105 — Right to Delete | Log `data.deleted` with consumer request ID and completion timestamp |
| §1798.106 — Right to Correct | Log `data.updated` with correction details and request tracking |
| §1798.120 — Right to Opt-Out | Log `consent.withdrawn` with opt-out type and scope |
| §1798.121 — Sensitive PI limits | Log `data.accessed` with sensitive data classification for enhanced auditing |
| §1798.150 — Security measures | Hash-chained immutable logs, WORM storage, encryption, tenant isolation |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for CCPA/CPRA:**
- **Consumer request tracking** — log every DSAR (data subject access request) with request ID and response timelines
- **Deletion verification** — immutable proof that deletion requests were fulfilled within the 45-day deadline
- **Consent management** — track opt-in/opt-out events with full audit trail
- **3-year retention** — meets CCPA record-keeping requirements with configurable retention

## FedRAMP (NIST SP 800-53)

FedRAMP requires cloud service providers to implement NIST 800-53 controls, with the AU (Audit & Accountability) family being central to audit logging requirements.

| FedRAMP Control | AuditRails Feature |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| AU-2 — Audit Events | Configurable event logging via SDK — `auth.login`, `access.granted`, `config.changed`, etc. |
| AU-3 — Content of Audit Records | Every record includes action, timestamp (ms), actor_id, resource, source IP, metadata |
| AU-8 — Time Stamps | Server-side UTC timestamps with millisecond precision |
| AU-9 — Protection of Audit Information | WORM storage (S3 Object Lock) + SHA-256 hash chaining prevent tampering |
| AU-11 — Audit Record Retention | Configurable retention up to 7 years (FedRAMP requires 3 years minimum) |
| AC-2/AC-6 — Account Management & Least Privilege | Log `access.granted`, `access.revoked`, `permission.granted` with roles |
| IR-4/IR-5/IR-6 — Incident Handling & Reporting | Log `incident.detected`, `incident.notified`, `incident.resolved` |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for FedRAMP:**
- **NIST AU-family coverage** — comprehensive audit logging with all required fields (AU-2, AU-3, AU-8)
- **Tamper-proof storage** — WORM + hash chaining satisfies AU-9 protection requirements
- **3-year retention** — meets FedRAMP minimum with configurable extension to 7 years
- **Continuous monitoring** — dashboard provides real-time audit record review and analysis (AU-6, AU-7)

## SOX — Sarbanes-Oxley Act

SOX requires public companies to maintain internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR) with IT General Controls being critical to compliance.

| SOX Requirement | AuditRails Feature |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| §302 — Internal controls certification | Immutable audit trail of all financial system changes supports CEO/CFO certification |
| §404 — Management assessment of ICFR | Compliance dashboard tracks control completion, CSV export for evidence packages |
| §802 — 7-year record retention | Subscribing to SOX (on Framework Trails or Compliance Trails) sets your retention to its required 7 years, enforced via WORM storage (S3 Object Lock COMPLIANCE mode) |
| §806 — Whistleblower protection | Log `compliance.report` events with anonymized tracking |
| ITGC — Logical access controls | Log `auth.login`, `auth.failed`, `access.granted`, `access.revoked` |
| ITGC — Segregation of duties | Log `permission.granted` with role metadata for duty separation auditing |
| Change Management — Authorization | Log `change.approved`, `config.changed`, `deploy.completed` with approval chain |

**Key AuditRails capabilities for SOX:**
- **7-year WORM retention** — meets §802 record retention with criminal-penalty-grade immutability
- **Change management auditing** — complete trail of who changed what, when, and who approved it
- **Segregation of duties** — role-based access logging enables audit of duty separation
- **Evidence export** — CSV export for §404 management assessment and external auditor attestation

## Also Active: EU AI Act & Country-Specific Frameworks

**EU AI Act** gets the deepest treatment of any framework — see the [Compliance page](https://auditrails.io/compliance) for the full Article 12/14/50/72/73 mapping, including which controls are auto-verified from ingest pattern alone.

The remaining 7 country-specific frameworks are also active and follow the same pattern (structured action catalog, auto-verified controls where possible, evidence export):

| Framework | Region | Focus |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| amm_sistema | Italy | System-administrator access logging (Garante) |
| Cyber Essentials | UK | NCSC-backed baseline security certification |
| BSI C5 | Germany | Cloud service provider security criteria |
| DNB Good Practice | Netherlands | Financial-sector IT risk (DORA-aligned) |
| SecNumCloud | France | ANSSI cloud sovereignty qualification |
| UK GDPR | UK | Post-Brexit UK data protection (ICO-enforced) |
| LOPDGDD | Spain | Spain's GDPR implementation, plus digital rights at work |

Enable any framework — including these — from **Settings → Compliance Frameworks** in the dashboard. Each ships with its own action catalog, control checklist, and (where applicable) auto-verification, exactly like the 10 frameworks detailed above.

## Getting Started with Compliance

1. **Enable the compliance dashboard** — available on Framework Trails and Compliance Trails (not on Starter Trails, which has no compliance frameworks)
2. **Review the checklist** for your framework — each control shows what's needed
3. **Start logging required events** — use the Action Catalog for SDK code snippets
4. **Run auto-verification** — AuditRails checks if required events are being logged
5. **Track progress** — category breakdowns show your compliance posture at a glance
